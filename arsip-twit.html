@S_Surprenant (Stéphane Surprenant) replied to Farmer:
Another good reason is that there is no econometric free lunch. Working in levels only gives you a convergent estimator for IRFs and FEVDs at short horizons (Phillips, 1998). If you apply max share at long horizons to get long-run restrictions without really using them as people do with news shocks, you have a problem (Guay, Pelgrin, and Surprenant, 2025).
